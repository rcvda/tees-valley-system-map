# Health Determinants Research Collaboration (HDRC) South Tees — team

**Source:** https://hdrcsouthtees.co.uk/meet-the-team
**Type:** Web page · **Captured:** 2026-07-25
NIHR-funded. Delivered by Teesside University with Public Health South Tees; RCVDA delivers the Community-led Research Programme within it. The HDRC team is situated in Public Health South Tees and reports to Mark Adams (Joint DPH); Dr Jan Lecouturier is Lead/Director; Professor Paul Crawshaw (Teesside University) is Academic Lead.
