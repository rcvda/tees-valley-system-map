# Redcar & Cleveland Borough Council — The Cabinet and Cabinet papers

**Source:** https://www.redcar-cleveland.gov.uk/councillors-and-committees/the-cabinet-and-cabinet-papers

The Cabinet is the part of the council responsible for most strategic decisions. It includes the leader, deputy leader, and seven councillors with portfolio responsibilities. Decisions are made by Cabinet, a committee of the Cabinet, individual Cabinet members, or officers under delegated powers.
